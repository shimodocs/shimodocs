## Built for collaboration. Designed for control.

ShimoDocs combines a Google Docs-like real-time collaboration experience with private deployment, enterprise permissions, and greater control over business data. Teams can create, edit, comment, review, and share content in one workspace—without passing attachments around or maintaining multiple file versions.

3. Email [many@shimo.im](mailto:many@shimo.im) to request a free 5-user license.
4. Activate the license, create a workspace, and invite your team.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Is ShimoDocs really free?</strong></summary>

Yes. Teams of up to 5 users can email [many@shimo.im](mailto:many@shimo.im) to request a free license, with no credit card required. Teams with more than 5 users can choose a paid plan based on team size.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Do I need my own server?</strong></summary>

Yes. ShimoDocs runs in your private environment, so your organization provides and manages the servers, private cloud, or other infrastructure. If you are not ready to deploy, you can start with an [online trial](https://shimodocs.com/#online-trial).

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How is ShimoDocs different from Google Docs?</strong></summary>

ShimoDocs provides a familiar real-time editing experience while running in your private environment. Your organization retains more control over data location, identity systems, access permissions, audit records, and integrations.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can we connect our existing AI services?</strong></summary>

Yes. ShimoDocs can connect to AI services selected by your organization for drafting, summarizing, rewriting, and translation, while letting your team manage AI usage according to its own policies.

</details>
